      text: |-
        ### 🧠 DeepSeg: brain tumor segmentation
        A modular encoder-decoder framework for automatic glioma segmentation from FLAIR MRI. It was the foundation for my later multimodal models, including the method that won **first place in the MICCAI BraTS 2022 challenge**.
        [Code](https://github.com/razeineldin/DeepSeg) · [Paper (IJCARS 2020)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-020-02186-z) · [BraTS 2022 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09310)

        ### 🔍 NeuroXAI and TransXAI: explainable AI for brain MRI
        Tools for explaining what deep networks see in brain-tumor MRI, for both classification and segmentation, and a hybrid vision-transformer/CNN model with built-in explanations.
        [NeuroXAI code](https://github.com/razeineldin/NeuroXAI) · [TransXAI code](https://github.com/razeineldin/TransXAI) · [Paper (IJCARS 2022)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-022-02619-x) · [Paper (Scientific Reports 2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-54186-7)

        ### 🧩 Slicer-DeepSeg: deep learning in 3D Slicer
        An open-source 3D Slicer extension that brings deep-learning brain tumor segmentation into the clinical planning workflow.
        [Code](https://github.com/razeineldin/Slicer-DeepSeg) · [Paper (CDBME 2021)](https://doi.org/10.1515/cdbme-2021-1007)

        ### 🩻 iRegNet and NeuroIGN: image-guided neurosurgery
        Deep learning registration of preoperative MRI to intraoperative ultrasound to compensate for brain shift, combined into NeuroIGN, an explainable multimodal image-guided system for brain tumor surgery.
        [iRegNet (IEEE Access 2021)](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2021.3120306) · [NeuroIGN (J. Med. Syst. 2024)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10916-024-02037-3)

        ### 🤖 AI for robot-assisted surgery and surgical training
        At FAU's SPARC lab: foundation models for surgical instrument segmentation (SAMSurg), natural-language anatomy localization for neuroendoscopy training (NeuroLens), and speech interfaces for surgical robots.
        [SAMSurg (IEEE Access 2024)](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3520386) · [Neuroendoscopy training (IEEE TBME 2024)](https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.2024.3405814) · [NeuroLens (IJCARS 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-025-03463-5)

        ### 🦯 Mubser: assistive technology for the visually impaired
        Co-founded startup (2012–2015) building assistive technology for blind users, such as FloosMubser, an Android app that recognizes banknotes with the phone camera. Finalist in the MIT Enterprise Forum, Pan Arab Region. My related research covered ground-plane and walkable-path detection for visually impaired navigation.
        [FloosMubser code](https://github.com/razeineldin/FloosMubser) · [Ground-plane detection (SAI 2016)](https://doi.org/10.1109/SAI.2016.7556009) · [Path detection (IEEE SMC 2017)](https://doi.org/10.1109/SMC.2017.8122809)
